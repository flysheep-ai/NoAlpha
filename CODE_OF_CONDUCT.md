# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in NoAlpha a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss evidence rather than people, and
accept constructive feedback. Disagreement about a model, metric, or research
claim is welcome when it is specific and reproducible.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the maintainer contact described
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Project maintainers may edit or remove comments,
commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy, and may ban
contributors for repeated or serious violations.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
